### 👋 About me

I'm a Machine Learning Engineer with an MSc in Computer Science from Ca' Foscari University of Venice. I take data-driven solutions from prototype to production: data pipelines, model training, LLM-based agents, RAG assistants, and deployed web and mobile apps. I'm research-oriented and passionate about AI in industrial contexts.

- 🛠️ **Now:** Freelance Software Engineer at [The Italian Elixir](https://zulle09.github.io/#experience) and Training Hub (TypeScript services, a Flask/htmx coaching SaaS shipped as PWA and Capacitor app)
- 🏭 **Before:** ML Engineer & Researcher at NAIS Engineering, working on anomaly detection for Formula 1 engine telemetry and SCADA dashboards
- 🎓 **Thesis:** Active Learning with Dynamical Systems, a graph-transduction approach to picking the hardest samples
- 💬 **Open to:** new ideas, collaborations and real-world problems. If you need help with any of my projects, reach out!
